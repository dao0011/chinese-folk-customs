Six ways into the household

Each group follows a place, material, or repeated part of the day rather than a fixed instruction.

Warm Foot Soaks Basins, water, and the end of the day. Kitchen Comforts Market goods, pots, and cups left nearby. Cloth Warmth Cloth, stored heat, and household care. Sleep & Calm Bedrooms, bedding, and evening objects. Everyday Rituals Small work repeated without ceremony. Seasonal Comfort Rooms and objects used as weather changes.
