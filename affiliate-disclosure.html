Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency matters. Here is how affiliate links work on Folk Calm.

Amazon Associates Program

Folk Calm is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, Folk Calm earns from qualifying purchases.

What This Means for You

  • When you click an affiliate link on this site and make a purchase on Amazon, we may earn a small commission — at no additional cost to you
  • The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go directly to Amazon
  • Commissions are paid by Amazon, not by you

Our Commitment

We only link to products that are historically and culturally relevant to the folk practices described in our articles. These are common household goods — wooden basins, dried herbs, cloth bags, kitchen items — not health products or supplements. A link does not constitute an endorsement or recommendation; it simply reflects that the item is associated with the described cultural practice.

Our editorial content is never influenced by affiliate relationships. Articles are written based on oral history, classical texts, and ethnographic research. Affiliate links are added after the article is complete, and only where a relevant product exists.

Identifying Affiliate Links

Affiliate links on Folk Calm are marked with rel="nofollow sponsored" attributes (as required by Google and the FTC). We also place a clear disclosure notice near any section containing affiliate links, and a site-wide disclosure appears in the footer of every page.

Other Affiliate Programs

Currently, Folk Calm only participates in the Amazon Associates program. If we join additional affiliate programs in the future, this page will be updated accordingly.
