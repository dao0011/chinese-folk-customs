Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up for our email newsletter or request our free cultural guide, we collect your email address. We do not collect any other personal information.

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts that collect personal data. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used exclusively for:

  • Delivering the free cultural guide you requested
  • Sending occasional newsletters with new articles and cultural content (typically 2-4 emails per month)
  • Notifying you about new free guides or resources

We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties.

3. Email Service Provider

We use Resend to deliver emails — including the free guide and occasional newsletters. When you submit your email address, it is processed through Resend's email API for delivery only. We do not store email addresses on Resend's servers beyond the time needed to send your message. You can review Resend's privacy policy at resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe — Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it to stop receiving emails immediately.
  • Access your data — Contact us to request a copy of the data we hold about you (your email address and subscription date).
  • Delete your data — Contact us to request permanent deletion of your data from our email list.

5. GDPR Compliance (EU/EEA Visitors)

For visitors from the European Union and European Economic Area:

  • We collect your email address only after you give explicit consent by checking the consent checkbox on our signup form
  • The legal basis for processing is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR)
  • You may withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing
  • Data is stored by ConvertKit, a US-based company compliant with EU-US data transfer frameworks

6. CCPA Compliance (California Visitors)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your data, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us via the email address listed on our About page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
