A short history

A history kept at home

The guide follows food traditions kept near the stove and folk practices remembered through ordinary household objects.

Written records

From old texts to the kitchen

Chinese food traditions moved between materia medica, seasonal calendars, regional cookbooks, and handwritten household notes. An ingredient described on the page could later appear as a plain drink or bowl prepared at home. The pages follow that quieter passage from written description to household use.

Household memory

Stories held by ordinary objects

Many folk practices survived through repeated household actions rather than formal instruction. A cloth, cup, basin, or warming vessel could carry a local custom from one generation to the next, with details changing from place to place. The guide records the histories around those actions and the settings in which they were remembered.

Reading note

This edition documents inherited household customs as cultural material. Historical practices are presented in their original domestic context and are not instructions for personal use.
